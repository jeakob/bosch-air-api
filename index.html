Mode

Auto turns the ring on while a window is active (and air is not good, if enabled), off otherwise. Paused never touches the ring, so the Bosch app is in charge. Auto, on and off undo changes made in the app within a minute.

Time windows (ring on)

Use HH:MM, sunrise, sunset, or offsets in minutes such as sunset+30 or sunrise-15. If the end is before the start, the window runs past midnight.

Location and time zone

Used only for sunrise and sunset; two decimals (about 1 km) are plenty.

Air quality

The purifier pushes sensor readings on its own schedule (often every 30 minutes). Until one arrives, air counts as not good, so the ring stays on in its windows.
