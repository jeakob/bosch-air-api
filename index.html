Mode

Auto turns the ring on while a window is active (windows marked "only if air not good" also need bad air), and outside the windows while air is not good if that option is on; off otherwise. Paused never touches the ring, so the Bosch app is in charge. Auto, on and off undo changes made in the app within a minute.

Location and time zone

Used only for sunrise and sunset; two decimals (about 1 km) are plenty.

Time windows (ring on)

Use HH:MM, sunrise, sunset, or offsets in minutes such as sunset+30 or sunrise-15. If the end is before the start, the window runs past midnight. A window turns the ring on whatever the air quality, unless Only if air not good is ticked.

Air quality

Good means at or below a number limit, or equal to a text limit. Readings come from the purifier's cloud history every 30 minutes; without one from the last 2 hours, air counts as good. History keeps 1 to 365 days; shortening it deletes older readings.
